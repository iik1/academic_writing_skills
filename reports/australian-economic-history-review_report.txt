HARD CONSTRAINTS IDENTIFIED
============================
1. Word Limit: 8,000-10,000 words (enforced; >12,000 words unlikely to be accepted)
2. Abstract: 100 words or fewer, non-structured
3. Citation Style: Harvard (author-date system)
4. Headings: Unnumbered, in capital letters
5. Section Organization: Logical flow (introduction, data/methodology, results, conclusions); flexibility in order
6. Mathematical Equations: Must be accessible to non-quantitative historians and intelligent lay readers
7. Data Availability Statement: Required
8. Peer Review: Double-blind (author anonymization required)
9. Reference Formatting: Free Format accepted; typesetter handles final formatting
