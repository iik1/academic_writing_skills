2. CITATION DENSITY (RELAXATION)

   - Authors cite empirical benchmarks within results tables and narratives
   - FIELD_SKILL specifies low-to-medium in results; JOR shows higher density

3. STRUCTURAL DEVIATIONS (PRACTICE ONLY)

   - Key Takeaways or boxed summaries of actionable findings present in some articles
   - Not mandated by guidelines but common in published articles

   - Practical policy recommendations accompanying empirical findings (implicit guideline enforcement)
   - Case-study framing and practitioner-centric presentation observed
   - Explicit in journal scope and articles reviewed
