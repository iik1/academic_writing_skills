   - Status: GUIDELINE-STATED + PRACTICE OBSERVED
   - Finding: This journal explicitly mandates robustness checks (alternative specifications,
     placebo tests, sensitivity analyses) in a dedicated section or labeled subsection.
     This is more heavily emphasized than in ABS 4* field baseline (FIELD_SKILL).
   - Delta classification: RELAXATION (ABS 2 journals typically relax standards; here it
     tightens on robustness transparency and reproducibility emphasis).
   - Evidence: Scope guidelines explicitly state "robustness checks as standard component";
     also emphasizes data access transparency and reproducibility.

2. MECHANISM IDENTIFICATION REQUIREMENT (ENFORCED)
   - Status: GUIDELINE-STATED (explicit scope rule)
   - Finding: Journal desk-rejects purely descriptive empirical analyses without identified
     theory or underlying mechanism(s). This conflicts with FIELD_SKILL norm, which accepts
     well-identified reduced-form work without mechanism hypothesis.
   - Delta classification: RELAXATION (scope-tightening: lower-tier journal raises bar on
     mechanistic identification to maintain rigor in narrower domain).
   - Evidence: Scope statement: "papers that document relationships between variables without
     identifying the theory or underlying mechanism(s) giving rise to these relationships"
     are explicitly out of scope.
