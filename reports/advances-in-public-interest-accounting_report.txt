HARD CONSTRAINTS (PUBLISHER GUIDELINES)
========================================
Structure:
  - Title, running head (≤65 chars), abstract, keywords, main text, acknowledgments, appendices, references
  - Numbered or formatted headings: Level 1 bold, Level 2 medium italics

Length:
  - 7,000–10,000 words per chapter (inclusive of abstract, text, references)

Abstract:
  - Maximum 250 words, single paragraph, unstructured
  - Must state research question, approach, key contributions

Keywords:
  - Up to 6 keywords, comma-separated, encapsulating principal topics

Citations and References:
  - Author-date format (APA 6th Edition, moving toward APA 7th)
  - In-text citations: (Author Year) or Author (Year)
  - Reference list at chapter end
  - Multiple citations: semicolon-separated, alphabetical or chronological
  - Direct quotes: must include page numbers

Tables and Figures:
  - Placed at chapter end or after references
  - Appendix numbering: A1, A2, A3; Appendix A, Appendix B if multiple appendices
  - Equations in Appendix A: A.1, A.2, etc.

Explicit Prohibitions:
  - No direct quotations from scholarly literature (paraphrase and cite)
  - Permissions required for copyrighted material
  - Copyright: Original work; Emerald requires non-exclusive rights (print, electronic, worldwide, English)
