=============== DELTAS FROM FIELD_SKILL ===============

DELTA 1: Word Limits
Classification: ENFORCED, RELAXATION
Description: ABS 2 journal enforces 9,000-word cap for articles and 2,500 for notes. Field baseline (ABS 4*/4) does not specify limits and implies 25,000–40,000-word papers with appendices are normal. This is a significant constraint requiring ruthless prioritization of results.
Impact: Empirical papers must compress robustness; appendices uncommon; online supplements count against word limit.
Observed in: Official guidelines, confirmed across recent issues.

DELTA 2: Notes Format
Classification: ENFORCED
Description: Field baseline assumes full-length articles. RDE actively solicits short notes (2,500 words) for methodological insights, policy findings, or comments on published work. This is not common in top-tier outlets.
Impact: Partial replication, narrow-scope studies, and critical comments are legitimate submissions.
Observed in: Official guidelines.

DELTA 3: Double-Blind Peer Review
Classification: ENFORCED
Description: Field baseline does not specify anonymity (varies by journal). RDE explicitly requires double-blind review with author anonymization. Standard at Wiley but explicit requirement.
Impact: Self-citations must be carefully rephrased to avoid identity leakage.
Observed in: Editorial policy statements.

DELTA 4: JEL Classification Codes
Classification: ENFORCED
Description: Field baseline does not mandate JEL codes. RDE requires them; they are metadata for routing and referee matching.
Impact: Incorrect JEL codes may route papers to unsuitable referees or trigger desk rejection.
Observed in: Official guidelines.

DELTA 5: Structural Flexibility
Classification: PRACTICE ONLY, RELAXATION
Description: Field baseline prescribes "near-universal" empirical and theory structures. RDE is more flexible; section ordering is advisory, not mandatory.
Impact: A paper can merge Results/Discussion, omit standalone Literature section, or reorder methods/background.
Observed in: "Manuscript Format" section states "no requirements for formatting you choose" and allows flexible section inclusion.
