DELTA 1: Voice and Person (RELAXATION)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Field Baseline Expectation:
  "First-person plural voice ('we test,' 'we argue,' 'we find') throughout,
   including in the theory and hypothesis sections" (CONSENSUS)

JEMI Observed Practice:
  Predominantly third-person passive ("data were collected") and impersonal active
  ("the findings demonstrate"). First-person plural reserved for methodological
  choices and key claims, not throughout. Consistent across all 5 articles.

Classification: RELAXATION
  ABS 1 journal deviates downward from field norm (field expects pervasive "we";
  JEMI accepts passive/impersonal throughout). No stated guideline restriction;
  practice uniformly passive across sample.

Evidence: All examined articles maintain third-person voice as default.
Czernek-Marszałek et al. (2026): "data were collected using CAWI";
Ha et al. (2025): "The results demonstrate...";
Urban & Govender (2024): "the analysis concluded."

Impact: Authors should expect passive/impersonal voice to be standard and
acceptable. Do not over-weight "we" as mandatory.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DELTA 2: Structured Abstract Format (ENFORCED)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Field Baseline Expectation:
  Field baseline makes no abstract format specification.

JEMI Requirement:
  Mandatory five-section abstract structure:
    PURPOSE — research aim/questions and identified gaps
    METHODOLOGY — sample, design, analytical approach
    FINDINGS — key results
    IMPLICATIONS — contributions to theory and practice
    ORIGINALITY/VALUE — significance and novelty

  Word count: 250–350 words. Section demarcations required (explicit labels or
  clear breaks).

Classification: ENFORCED (journal-specific, no field precedent)
  Explicitly stated in guidelines and uniformly observed in all 5 articles.

Evidence: Every article examined adheres exactly. Ha et al. (2025) abstract
includes "PURPOSE: [text] METHODOLOGY: [text]..." with explicit labels.
Average length ~300 words, within range.

Impact: Non-compliance risk. Missing or combined sections will trigger editorial
requests for revision before review. Required for submission.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DELTA 3: Citation Style & DOI (ENFORCED)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Field Baseline Expectation:
  No specific citation format mandated in field baseline.

JEMI Requirement:
  APA 7th edition mandatory. DOI required for all sources where available,
  formatted as clickable hyperlink: https://doi.org/[number]. Free DOI lookup
  via Crossref. All articles strictly follow APA parenthetical author-date style.

Classification: ENFORCED (journal-specific, guideline-stated)
  Guidelines explicitly mandate APA 7th and DOI. All examined articles comply
  uniformly. DOI inclusion non-negotiable.

Impact: Missing DOI or non-APA format will trigger editorial rejection.
Pre-check all references via Crossref before submission.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DELTA 4: Multi-Study Designs (PRACTICE VARIATION—Lower Prevalence at This Tier)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Field Baseline Expectation:
  "Multi-study designs (e.g., a primary study plus a supplementary/replication
   study using an independent or archival sample) are a well-established
   structural option for strengthening causal or construct validity claims, not a
   deviation from norm" (CONSENSUS at ABS 4*/4 level).

JEMI Observed Practice:
  All 5 examined articles employ single-study designs. Zero multi-study papers
  observed. This is consistent with ABS 1 journals being lower-tier venues where
  multi-study requirements are less uniformly expected.

Classification: PRACTICE ONLY (observed absence, not stated prohibition)
  No guideline prohibits multi-study designs; they simply do not appear in this
  sample. Likely reflects journal tier rather than explicit policy.

Impact: Authors should not assume multi-study designs are expected. Single-study
empirical papers are the norm. Multi-study papers may attract attention, but
single-study rigor is standard.

================================================================================
COMMON PITFALLS (JEMI-SPECIFIC)
================================================================================

1. Unstructured or incomplete abstract. The five-part format (PURPOSE|METHODOLOGY|
   FINDINGS|IMPLICATIONS|ORIGINALITY) is non-negotiable. Partial abstracts or
   failure to clearly demarcate sections risks desk rejection.

2. Missing DOI in references. APA 7th with mandatory DOI is a hard constraint.
   Every source must include DOI if available; blank DOIs will trigger rejection.

3. Manuscript length non-compliance. 8,000–12,000 words (main text only) is
   strictly enforced. Papers substantially over or under will be returned prior
   to review.

4. Failure to disclose AI tool use. Guidelines explicitly mandate declaration of
   AI use in preparation. Undisclosed AI raises ethical concerns and may
   invalidate peer review.

5. Missing data/code availability statements. JEMI requires explicit statements
   specifying where data/code can be accessed (repository URL) or that they are
   proprietary/restricted. Vague "available upon request" language delays
   acceptance.
