DELTAS FOUND: 5 major + context
Dimensions affected:
1. Voice and Register (RELAXATION): Third-person/passive voice more prevalent than field baseline "we" dominance
2. Hypothesis Development (RELAXATION): Not always a separate section; integrated into Introduction/Theory
3. Practical Implications (RELAXATION): Not consistently expected as dedicated subsection
4. Citation Density (PRACTICE ONLY): Abstracts contain minimal citations; full-text density not reliably assessed
5. Article Types (EXPANSION): Explicit welcome of literature reviews alongside empirical/conceptual work
6. Submission Format (HARD CONSTRAINT): Microsoft Word only; no LaTeX/PDF alternatives

FIELD_SKILL OVERRIDES:
- Voice/person: IRE tolerates third-person/impersonal constructions; field baseline expects first-person plural throughout
- Hypothesis Development: Not mandatory as explicit section; may be embedded
- Practical Implications: Not a near-universal subsection requirement
- All other dimensions (sentence style, argumentation, results reporting, discussion structure, hedging location, citation engagement) align with FIELD_SKILL or have insufficient evidence of deviation

HARD CONSTRAINTS IDENTIFIED:
- Abstract: 150 words maximum (non-negotiable per guidelines)
- Keywords: Required, placed below abstract
- Formatting: 12pt, double-spaced, A4, Microsoft Word
- Citation style: Alphabetical, full journal titles (no acronyms), include volume/issue/year/pages/DOI where available
- Tables/figures: Must be created in Word natively; no cut-and-paste from PDF/JPEG/GIF
- Diagrams: Word-native only; placement marked with notation (e.g., "{Table 1.2 near here}")
- Footnotes: Preferred over endnotes
- Anonymity: All author-identifying information must be removed from abstract and body for blind review
- Submission: Email to both vanstela@tcd.ie and andrew.burke@tcd.ie

ACCESS LIMITATIONS:
The journal is fully open-access from 2023 onward. No paywalls encountered. Attempted direct fetches of 2 older articles (Vol. 21, 2023) returned 404 errors (likely stale links in archive), but sufficient 2024–2025 articles were accessible to support delta analysis. No institutional authentication barriers encountered.

FIELD_SKILL VALIDATION:
The FIELD_SKILL baseline (ABS 4*/4 norms) is appropriately conservative. IRE, as an ABS 2 journal, relaxes several norms (first-person plural not universal, practical implications optional, hypothesis sections not required) while maintaining rigor in methods transparency and structured argumentation. The lower tier explains the deltas; they are consistent with ABS 2 positioning.

NOTES FOR USER:
This skill is calibrated for ABS 2 standards and should not be mistaken for field-leading norms. Authors familiar with ABS 4*/4 journals may find IRE more flexible on voice and section structure but must still maintain methodological rigor and clarity. The 150-word abstract limit is unusually strict and warrants special attention during revision.
