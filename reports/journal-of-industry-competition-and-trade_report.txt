HARD CONSTRAINTS EXTRACTED (Author Guidelines)
----------------------------------------------
1. LENGTH:
   - Target: < 20 double-spaced pages
   - Maximum: 40 pages (absolute limit)

2. ABSTRACT:
   - Required: Yes
   - Word count: 150–250 words (strict bounds)
   - Format: Unstructured prose, no citations/abbreviations
   - Keywords listed separately below abstract

3. CITATION STYLE:
   - Author-year (Harvard, parenthetical or in-text)
   - In-text formats: (Author Year), (Author & Author Year), (Author et al. Year)
   - Bibliography examples extracted and documented

4. STRUCTURE:
   - Decimal heading numbering (1, 1.1, 1.2, 2, 2.1, etc.)
   - Max three heading levels
   - Standard empirical/theory structure: Intro, Literature/Background,
     Data/Methods, Analysis, Conclusion, Appendix

5. TABLES/FIGURES:
   - Tables embedded in text (not end of document)
   - Use word processor table function (not tabs/spaces)
   - Captions required; notes must explain methodology, sample restrictions
   - Consecutive numbering (Table 1, Table 2, Figure 1, etc.)

6. PROHIBITIONS:
   - Theoretical papers without empirical/policy implications rejected
   - Purely institutional descriptions not connected to theory rejected
