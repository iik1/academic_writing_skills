DELTAS FOUND: 10 major dimensions with differences from FIELD_SKILL baseline

1. ABSTRACT STRUCTURE (ENFORCED)
   - Mandatory 7 labeled sections: Orientation, Research purpose, Motivation, Research design, Main findings, Practical implications, Contribution/value-add
   - Past tense required
   - No citations
   - Non-negotiable format

2. WORD LIMIT (ENFORCED)
   - 7,000 words max for original research (vs. 10,000–15,000 in typical MGMT journals)
   - Significant constraint on literature engagement and methods detail

3. REFERENCE CEILING (ENFORCED)
   - Maximum 60 references (vs. 40–80 typical in FIELD_SKILL)
   - Restrictive selective citation requirement

4. CITATION STYLE (ENFORCED)
   - Harvard referencing required (not APA)
   - DOIs must be verified via CrossRef
   - Format: (Author Year) in text

5. LANGUAGE VARIANT (ENFORCED)
   - British English per Oxford English Dictionary
   - Affects spelling, vocabulary, date format

6. TABLE/FIGURE LIMIT (ENFORCED)
   - Maximum 7 total (restrictive for data-heavy studies)
   - All must be cited in legend, text, and reference list

7. ETHICAL APPROVAL DOCUMENTATION (ENFORCED)
   - Formal documents required as submission materials, not optional
   - Approval number, committee name, consent method, confidentiality measures

8. AI TECHNOLOGY DISCLOSURE (ENFORCED)
   - Explicit declaration of tools used (e.g., ChatGPT, generative models)
   - Specific tool names required

9. CRITICAL THEORY PROHIBITION (ENFORCED)
   - Research grounded in Critical Theory explicitly rejected
   - Unique, highly restrictive prohibition

10. RESULTS/DISCUSSION SEPARATION (ENFORCED)
    - Must be two separate, clearly labeled sections
    - Not optional combination

FIELD_SKILL OVERRIDES: 10
- Abstract: Structured only (not unstructured option)
- Word limit: Much tighter (7,000 vs. 10,000–15,000)
- References: Ceiling of 60 (vs. baseline 40–80)
- Language: British English specified
- Tables/figures: 7 max (more restrictive)
- Ethical documentation: Compulsory as submission materials
- AI disclosure: Required (not in baseline)
- Critical Theory: Explicitly prohibited
- Results/Discussion: Must separate
- Citation style: Harvard (vs. APA)

OVERALL ASSESSMENT:
Acta Commercii imposes substantially tighter constraints than typical ABS 1 management journals,
particularly on word limit (7,000 vs. 10,000+), reference count (60 vs. 40–80), and abstract format
(mandatory structured template). The Critical Theory prohibition is unusual and requires careful
attention to epistemological framing. Otherwise, the journal follows standard management research
conventions for quantitative and qualitative work. British English and strict Harvard referencing
are non-negotiable requirements.
