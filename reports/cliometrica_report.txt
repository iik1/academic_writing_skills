HARD CONSTRAINTS IDENTIFIED
===========================

1. CITATION STYLE (ENFORCED)
   - Author-year parenthetical format strictly required
   - Examples: (Author Year); (Author1 Year; Author2 Year); et al. for 3+ authors
   - Applies to all article types; field baseline does not mandate specific style
   - This is more prescriptive than field norm

2. COMPLIANCE STATEMENT (ENFORCED)
   - Separate section "Compliance with Ethical Standards" mandatory
   - Must cover: funding sources, conflicts of interest (financial and non-financial)
   - Human subjects consent and animal welfare statements if applicable
   - Field baseline does not address this; journal-specific requirement

3. TABLE AND FIGURE FORMATTING (GUIDELINE ONLY)
   - Tables: Arabic numerals, consecutive in-text citation, caption with source
   - Figures: Electronic submission, EPS (vector) or TIFF (halftones) format
   - Size specifications per Springer standard (84mm or 174mm width)
   - Not uniformly enforced across sample but stated in guidelines

4. SUPPLEMENTARY MATERIALS POLICY (Type 3, Social Sciences)
   - Data availability statement mandatory
   - Data sharing encouraged but not required
   - Field baseline does not address; modern standard

5. NO EXPLICIT WORD/PAGE/ABSTRACT LENGTH LIMITS
   - Accessible guidelines do not specify manuscript length
   - Abstract format: compact, single-paragraph, non-structured (~100-150 words observed)
   - No formal constraint identified

CONCLUSION
==========

Cliometrica follows BUS HIST & ECON HIST field norms closely. Main journal-specific deviations:
- Mandatory author-year parenthetical citation format (more prescriptive than field)
- Required "Compliance with Ethical Standards" section (modern research governance)
