HARD CONSTRAINTS CONFIRMED
===========================

From Emerald house guidelines and journal homepage:
- Manuscript length: 1,000-4,000 words (Emerald standard)
- Abstract format: Structured (Purpose, Design/methodology/approach, Findings, etc.)
- Abstract word limit: 250 words maximum
- Keywords: 6 maximum
- Citation format: Author-year parenthetical (standard finance)
- Originality: Must not be previously published
- Plagiarism: Crossref Similarity Check applied
- AI use: Copy-editing with generative AI permitted (Emerald policy)
- Conflict of interest: Must be declared
