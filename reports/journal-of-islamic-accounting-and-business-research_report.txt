DELTAS FOUND (4)
----------------------------------------

1. ABSTRACT STRUCTURE [ENFORCED DELTA]
   - FIELD_SKILL: "short, structured summary" in "a single paragraph"
   - JIABR: Mandatory Emerald structured format with four labeled sections (Purpose, Design/methodology/approach, Findings, Originality/value), max 250 words
   - Delta: Multi-section format is hard constraint, not optional

2. MANUSCRIPT STRUCTURE FLEXIBILITY [RELAXATION]
   - FIELD_SKILL: Prescribes rigid canonical structure (Intro, Theory, Methods, Results, Discussion) as "near-universal"
   - JIABR: Explicitly accepts case studies, practice-based papers, and non-traditional structures
   - Delta: Less restrictive than ABS 4*/4 norms; flexibility prioritized over template conformity

3. SUBMISSION MECHANICS [ENFORCED DELTA]
   - FIELD_SKILL: Silent on submission platform
   - JIABR: Manuscripts must use ScholarOne via Emerald (no email, no third-party platforms)
   - Delta: Journal-specific technical constraint not covered by field baseline

4. ANONYMITY PROCEDURES [ENFORCED DELTA]
   - FIELD_SKILL: Silent on double-blind specifics
   - JIABR: Strict anonymity rules; author names removed from text, figures, captions; "previous research has shown" phrasing required; separate author file submission required
   - Delta: Explicit procedural requirement for review process
