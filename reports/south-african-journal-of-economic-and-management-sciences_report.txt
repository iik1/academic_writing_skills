JOURNAL PROFILE NOTES
================================================================================

AOSIS is a South African publishing collective. SAJEMS is the group's flagship
economics and management journal. The journal emphasizes Southern African
regional perspectives and applied research over pure theory. Scope includes
accounting, finance, business management, economics, and related fields.
Publication model is open-access with author-pays article processing charges
upon acceptance.

The journal maintains recent editorial innovations:
- Mandatory CRediT contributor roles (as of November 2025)
- Mandatory AI disclosure if tools were used in manuscript preparation
- Encouragement of data availability statements and open data practice
- Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) tagging of published articles

These align with current scholarly publishing norms but exceed FIELD_SKILL's
scope.
