  Journal explicitly prohibits generative AI copywriting but permits copy-editing.

  While FIELD_SKILL lists Practical/Managerial Implications as a "near-universal,
  substantial subsection," this journal elevates it further. All 8 sampled articles
  include explicit Policy or Practical Implications sections, often as dedicated
  subsections distinct from the main Discussion. Reflects journal's explicit
  policy-entrepreneurship nexus focus. This is a matter of emphasis and consistency,
  not a fundamental structural deviation.

DELTA 4: Results Section Tone (PRACTICE ONLY, aligns with FIELD_SKILL but consistent)
  All empirical articles report findings in direct, unhedged language ("Results
  indicate," "Findings reveal") with minimal interpretive framing. Interpretation
  strictly deferred to Discussion. This aligns with FIELD_SKILL norm but is
  observed uniformly across all sampled articles—consistent enough to reinforce
  in journal-specific guidance.

HARD CONSTRAINTS IDENTIFIED (from Author Guidelines & Practice)
================================================================================

2. Structure: IMRaD (or thematic for conceptual); clear heading hierarchy
3. Discussion: Must include Contributions, Practical/Policy Implications, Limitations
4. Self-citation: Use "previous research has demonstrated," not "our previous work"
5. Anonymity: Remove all author-identifying information from text and figures
6. Generative AI: Copywriting prohibited; copy-editing permitted
7. Length: No explicit cap; observed range 15–30 pages (typical 18–25)
8. References: APA style (parenthetical citations, alphabetized list)
