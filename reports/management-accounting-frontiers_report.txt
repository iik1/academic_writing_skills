CONCLUSION
==========
Management Accounting Frontiers is an ABS 1 journal (lower-tier) with a mixed academic/practitioner
audience. It relaxes some field conventions (length, audience formality) while maintaining core ACCOUNT
field norms (voice, methods rigor, citation style, results reporting).

The journal's primary distinguishing feature is its explicit use of numbered section structure (1, 2, 3, etc.)
and acceptance of accessible, application-oriented writing. These are documented in the SKILL.md as
PRACTICE-level deviations and RELAXATIONS respectively.

The inability to parse full PDFs and access the complete author guidelines is a limitation, but the
evidence gathered is sufficient to identify the key deltas that differentiate MAF writing from the
FIELD_SKILL baseline.
