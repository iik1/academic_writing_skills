NOTES FOR FUTURE REFERENCE
===========================

1. This journal is ABS 2 (lower tier), which explains structural flexibility relative to ABS 4*/4 baseline. The field baseline was derived exclusively from top-tier journals (The Accounting Review, JAE, JAR, AOS at ABS 4*/4 level). IJAIS acceptable deviations reflect this tier difference.

2. The journal's scope explicitly emphasizes information technology applications in accounting. This thematic focus is not a writing convention delta but a domain shift. Writers should note that purely non-IT accounting research may be out of scope regardless of methodological rigor.

3. The "Your Paper Your Way" Elsevier philosophy is shared across most Elsevier journals. IJAIS does not impose more rigid formatting than Elsevier's standard.

4. Keyword requirement (1-7) should be included in all submissions. This is sometimes overlooked by accounting researchers trained in ABS 4* journals where keywords are less emphasized.

5. The journal welcomes research notes and comments (shorter submissions). This is a genuine delta from the ABS 4* baseline and opens opportunities for rapid-turnaround contributions.
