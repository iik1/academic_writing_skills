DELTAS FOUND

Total dimensions with deltas: 7
Total dimensions with no delta: 9

Deltas by category:

1. HARD CONSTRAINTS (Enforced)
   - Target word length: 4,000 words (vs. field estimate of 3,000–7,500)
   - Abstract format: Non-technical, unstructured, exactly 160 words
   - Introduction structure: Exactly 4 required elements (question, industry importance, solution type, roadmap)
   - Target audience: Practitioners, not academics; plain English requirement explicit
   - Exhibits: Called "Exhibits" (not tables/figures); color + B&W distinguishability required
   - Copyright: Exclusive worldwide assignment with severe distribution restrictions
   - Font/spacing: Specific (12pt Times New Roman, single-spaced with 12pt space before paragraphs)

2. VOICE AND REGISTER (Enforced)
   - Practitioner-first, not academic-first
   - Explicit prohibition on jargon ("minimize technical jargon")
   - Papers written "primarily for academics" desk-rejected

3. CONCLUSION (Enforced)
   - Must be "brief summary reflecting abstract content"
   - Tighter than FIELD_SKILL's 1–4 paragraph standard

4. CITATION FORMAT (Enforced but standard)
   - Chicago Manual of Style 17th Edition, Chapter 15 (author-date)
   - Consistent with FIELD_SKILL expectation

5. COPYRIGHT AND REPRODUCIBILITY (Enforced)
   - Exclusive assignment (restrictive vs. higher-tier journals)
   - SSRN/ResearchGate posting prohibited without permission
   - Lower pressure for immediate reproducibility

6. INTRODUCTION STRUCTURE (Enforced, more rigid)
   - FIELD_SKILL: 8-move typical structure, with literature and roadmap
   - JWM: Exactly 4 elements; academic positioning explicitly prohibited

7. ABSTRACT STRUCTURE (Enforced, unique)
   - Unstructured, plain-language, practitioner-facing
   - Typical finance abstracts may include methodological detail; JWM prohibits this

FIELD_SKILL OVERRIDES
=====================

The following field-level norms are overridden by JWM hard constraints:

1. Technical density: FIELD_SKILL expects "high, domain-specific vocabulary used freely"; JWM requires justification and plain-language explanation for all technical terms.

2. Audience assumption: FIELD_SKILL targets "reviewers in this field" with econometric competence; JWM targets portfolio managers, accountants, lawyers, advisors with high domain knowledge of wealth management but not necessarily econometrics.

3. Introduction framing: FIELD_SKILL allows academic positioning (literature contribution, theory extension); JWM prohibits academic-first framing at desk stage.

4. Copyright and distribution: FIELD_SKILL assumes open-science norms (data packages, preprints); JWM enforces exclusive rights and restricts preprint posting.

5. Conclusion scope: FIELD_SKILL allows future-research discussion and extended limitation acknowledgment; JWM mandates brief summary tied to abstract.
