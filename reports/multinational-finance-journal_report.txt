HARD CONSTRAINTS EXTRACTED FROM GUIDELINES
===========================================
- Maximum 13,000 words (including all text, abstract, references, figures, tables, appendices)
- Abstract: ≤150 words, single paragraph
- Keywords: 4–5 keywords + JEL codes (below abstract)
- Citation format: Author-year parenthetical
- Structure recommendation: Introduction → Literature Review → Methodology → Results and Discussion → Conclusions
- Headings: numbered bold; subheadings italicized with letters; sub-subheadings italicized without numbers
- Tables/figures: numbered consecutively, placed after references, must be self-explanatory
- Format: Microsoft Word
- Equations: numbered consecutively, right-justified
- Language: English (American or British, not mixed); no grammar/spelling errors
- Submission fee: €50 non-refundable
- Review: Double-blind peer review
- Open access: Creative Commons CC-BY-NC

COMMON PITFALLS DOCUMENTED
===========================
Four MFJ-specific submission mistakes identified:
1. Exceeding 13,000-word limit (desk-rejection risk)
2. Placing tables/figures in-text instead of after references
3. Mixing British and American English
4. Insufficient robustness treatment in main text (deferring all to appendix with constrained space)
