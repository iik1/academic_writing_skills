DELTAS FROM FIELD SKILL BASELINE
=================================

Delta 1: MANUSCRIPT LENGTH REDUCTION (ENFORCED / RELAXATION)
- Field baseline assumes full-length empirical articles
- JBV Insights: 12 pages maximum (hard constraint)
- Implication: Methods, Results, Discussion sections must be substantially abbreviated
- Rationale: Companion journal positioning; rapid publication model

Delta 2: EXPLICIT WELCOME FOR PRELIMINARY/INCOMPLETE WORK (GUIDELINE ONLY)
- Scope explicitly welcomes "unusual findings, atheoretical descriptions, non-findings, replications"
- Field baseline assumes complete hypothesis-testing spine and theoretical explication
- Implication: Authors need not provide full Hypothesis Development, construct measurement detail, or extensive robustness checks
- Classification: RELAXATION

Delta 3: UNSTRUCTURED ABSTRACT (ENFORCED)
- Field baseline does not specify abstract structure
- JBV Insights requires unstructured format (250 words max)
- Implication: Different prose approach; no labeled sections; emphasis on flow and brevity

Delta 4: HIGHLIGHTS REQUIREMENT (ENFORCED)
- Field baseline does not mention highlights
- JBV Insights requires 3-5 bullet points (max 85 chars each) at submission
- Implication: Distinct supplementary requirement; practitioner-focused language expected

Delta 5: RAPID PUBLICATION EMPHASIS (GUIDELINE ONLY)
- 3-month turnaround target
- Field baseline does not specify timeline
- Implication: Expect streamlined review; avoid overly complex submissions inviting extended revision
