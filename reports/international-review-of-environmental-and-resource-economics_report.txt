DELTA ANALYSIS
==============

Major Finding: FUNDAMENTAL GENRE DELTA
  This journal is a literature review / survey publication, NOT a venue for original empirical or theory research. This directly contradicts the field baseline (econ-field-writing-style), which assumes empirical or theoretical papers with novel findings.

Deltas Identified (7 total):

1. [ENFORCED] Journal scope is literature reviews only, not empirical/theory papers
   - Stated in guidelines: "The journal publishes literature reviews and tutorial papers. Original research papers will not be considered unless they contain a major literature review or can be turned into a literature review with the original case study as an illustration."
   - Confirmed in all 14 spot-checked articles: every article is a literature review/survey
   - Field baseline violation: field baseline assumes papers generate novel data/theory; this journal inverts that expectation

2. [ENFORCED] Structured abstract required: 4 fields, 100 words each (400 maximum)
   - Stated in guidelines explicitly
   - Not covered by field baseline (field baseline discusses unstructured abstracts only)
   - Hard constraint override

3. [ENFORCED] Mandatory DOI inclusion in references
   - Stated: "Where a DOI is available, this should be included at the end of the reference"
   - Field baseline does not prescribe DOI inclusion explicitly
   - Citation format otherwise aligns (author-year)

4. [ENFORCED] Literature review scope precludes "Data," "Results," "Robustness" sections
   - Follows from journal scope constraint (delta #1)
   - Field baseline expects these sections in empirical papers; reviews do not have them
   - Structural consequence: review papers organized by theme/debate/methodology, not by data generation and analysis pipeline

5. [PRACTICE CONFIRMED] Citation density notably higher in literature synthesis sections
   - Observed across all 14 articles
   - Field baseline prescribes "high" introduction citation, "low" results citation
   - Review articles cite heavily throughout synthesis sections (5–15 citations per page in dense sections)

6. [ENFORCED] Graphics format specification: Encapsulated PostScript (EPS) preferred
   - Stated in guidelines; field baseline does not specify format
   - Not a dominant delta (standard practice in economics publishing circa 2024-2025)

7. [ENFORCED] Alphabetical reference order
   - Stated in guidelines
   - Field baseline does not prescribe alphabetical vs. citation order; author-year format already implies alphabetical convention
   - Explicit statement reinforces practice but not a major deviation
