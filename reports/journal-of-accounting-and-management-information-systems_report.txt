   numerals.

COMMON PITFALLS (Journal-Specific)
================================================================================

1. Exceeding word limits (most common): Papers drafted for ABS 4*/4 venues at
   12,000-15,000 words must be trimmed to fit 5,000-10,000 range.

2. Color in figures: Figures submitted in color will be rejected. All figures must
   use grayscale (black, white, grey) and patterns, not color.

3. Overly long abstracts: Abstracts exceeding 250 words are grounds for desk
   rejection. Ensure word count is strictly enforced.

4. Unstructured abstracts: Abstract must include both research objective and
   contribution; vague objectives cause desk rejection.

5. Blind review violations: Author-identifying information in manuscript body or
   headers results in desk rejection.
