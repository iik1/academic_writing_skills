FIELD BASELINE OVERRIDES
The following ENFORCED deltas override entrepreneurship-field-writing-style baseline:

1. VOICE AND PERSON (ENFORCED DELTA)
   - IJSMES requires: Third-person passive, impersonal voice throughout (even in methods)
   - FIELD_SKILL expects: First-person plural active ("we find," "we test")
   - Example violation: "We collected data..." should be "Data were collected..."
   - Severity: FUNDAMENTAL — This is the most significant deviation from the field baseline.

2. AUTHOR CONTRIBUTIONS AND CONFLICTS OF INTEREST (ENFORCED DELTA)
   - IJSMES requires: Mandatory Author Contributions (CRediT taxonomy) and Conflicts of Interest sections
   - FIELD_SKILL: Does not list as required structural elements
   - Status: Hard constraint per guidelines; appears in all sampled articles

3. HEDGING AND FORWARD-LOOKING RHETORIC (ENFORCED DELTA)
   - IJSMES practice: Minimal "future research should..." hedging; discussion closes with actionable recommendations
   - FIELD_SKILL consensus: Forward-looking hedges are "a standard and expected close to nearly every limitation"
   - Status: Observed consistently across all 12 articles; reflects lower-tier, applied orientation

4. DISCUSSION STRUCTURE AND EXTENT (PRACTICE ONLY / STRONG PATTERN)
   - IJSMES pattern: Brief, action-oriented discussion (policy/managerial recommendations)
   - FIELD_SKILL expectation: Extended, exploratory discussion with substantial Limitations and Future Research subsections
   - Observed in: All 12 articles; appears systematic rather than individual choice

PRACTICE-ONLY DELTAS (observed but not stated in guidelines)

1. CITATION DENSITY
   - Empirical articles: 14–30 sources (vs. FIELD_SKILL norm of 30–50+)
   - Review articles: 40–50+ sources
   - Status: RELAXATION (lower ABS tier permits leaner citation)

2. THEORETICAL LENS FRAMING
   - IJSMES pattern: Articles often omit explicit named theoretical lens in introduction
   - FIELD_SKILL expectation: "Framing a study's theoretical lens up front... is common and expected"
   - Alternative positioning: JEL classification codes used to situate work
   - Status: Consistent pattern but not enforced; reflects international/applied scope

3. CANON ENGAGEMENT AND SELF-CITATION
   - IJSMES: Broader international citations, less repetition of foundational entrepreneurship theorists
   - FIELD_SKILL: "Self-citation and citation of the same lead theorists recur heavily"
   - Status: PRACTICE ONLY; reflects journal's global readership
