DELTAS FROM FIELD_SKILL BASELINE: 4 major findings

1. ENFORCED: Empirical Structure Specificity
   - Journal explicitly requires: Introduction → Theoretical Background & State of Research → Methods → Results → Discussion → Conclusion
   - More prescriptive than FIELD_SKILL (which allows integration of intro and theory review)
   - This two-section split is non-negotiable for empirical papers

2. ENFORCED: Explicit Prohibition on Atheoretical Evaluations
   - Journal will not accept evaluations or empirical studies that focus solely on testing a training programme or model without wider contextual discussion and conceptual engagement
   - This is a stated editorial policy that differs from FIELD_SKILL baseline
   - Represents a rigorous stance on theory-practice integration

3. ENFORCED: Hard Constraints on Abstract Format
   - Maximum 350 words (unstructured)
   - No references or equations
   - Approximately 5 keywords in alphabetical order

4. PRACTICE ONLY (Relaxation): Free-Format Submission
   - Newer submission policy allows single-file submission (text, figures, tables combined)
   - This is a relaxation from traditional separate-file requirements
   - Core structure remains unchanged

OVERALL ASSESSMENT
==================

This is a lower-tier (ABS 2) journal that maintains rigorous editorial standards around theory-practice integration. It accepts both quantitative and qualitative empirical work, as well as conceptual and policy-oriented papers. The explicit rejection of atheoretical evaluations suggests the journal is positioned to resist purely instrumental or applied-only submissions, maintaining scholarly rigor even at a lower impact tier.
