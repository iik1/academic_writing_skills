CONCLUSION

Review of Managerial Science is an ABS 2, Springer-published management journal with
broad scope and openness to diverse methodologies. Its main deviation from the MGMT
field baseline is the explicit requirement for decimal heading numbering and slightly
greater prominence of systematic reviews and bibliometric work. Citation style, voice,
argumentation, and overall structure are field-standard. The journal is less prescriptive
on manuscript length than top-tier outlets, consistent with its ABS 2 ranking.

The SKILL.md file records these findings concisely, with FIELD_DEFAULT entries for all
dimensions with no deviation and explicit delta classification labels for the few areas
where RMS differs from the field baseline.
