RECOMMENDATIONS FOR AUTHORS
===========================

- Expect to write longer abstracts (~600 words) in narrative form
- Be transparent about data sourcing, sampling, and methodological choices
- Use general-to-specific approach if variable selection is exploratory; justify clearly
- Include extensive appendices for detailed data breakdowns
- Hedge not just causal claims but the entire research framing (acknowledge
  methodological limitations, novel contexts, and incomplete knowledge state)
- Avoid purely conceptual, bibliometric, or literature-review-only papers
- Focus on strong empirical quantitative work; qualitative must be very high quality
