     likely driven by the strict 100-word abstract cap rather than a distinct field practice.
     PRACTICE ONLY / RELAXATION.
  3. Structural Deviations - journal accepts brief notes (<=2,500 words), book reviews, and
     invited comment/tribute pieces in addition to standard research articles. GUIDELINE-
     CONFIRMED.
  4. Other Journal-Specific Patterns - narrower, more applied topical/geographic scope
     (Spain/Latin America agri-environmental policy) than typical FIELD_SKILL journals; noted
     as scope observation, not a style-altering delta.
