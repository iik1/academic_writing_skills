CONCLUSION
================================================================================

CESifo Economic Studies follows the ECON field baseline in voice, citation,
hedging, and statistical reporting norms. The principal deltas are:

1. LOWER BARRIER TO ENTRY (20–25 pages vs. longer ABS 4*/4 papers)
2. EXPLICIT STRUCTURAL FLEXIBILITY (no mandatory section sequence)
3. FORMAL TECHNICAL CONSTRAINTS (Harvard author-date, British English, specific
   table/figure formats)

These reflect the ABS 2 tier: the journal is less demanding in length and
architectural rigor, but more explicit in formatting conventions. Writers
transitioning from ABS 4*/4 to CESifo should reduce expected length, relax
section order rigidity, and confirm formatting details before submission.

The journal's closure (December 2024) limits the practical utility of this
skill, but the guidelines remain clear and may be historically relevant for
scholars reviewing CESifo archives or preparing submissions to successor
publications within the CESifo network.
