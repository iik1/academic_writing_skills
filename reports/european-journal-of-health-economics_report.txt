DELTAS FROM FIELD_SKILL (econ-field-writing-style)
---------------------------------------------------
Found: 5 major deltas

1. VOICE AND REGISTER [ENFORCED]
   - Field baseline: "we" + active voice (near-universal)
   - EJHE practice: Passive voice dominates ("was conducted," "were identified")
   - "We" appears mainly in Methods but not default throughout
   - Impact: Authors must adopt biomedical impersonal register, not economics "we show" style

2. CITATION FORMAT [ENFORCED]
   - Field baseline: Author-year parenthetical (e.g., Smith 2020)
   - EJHE practice: Numbered in-bracket [1], [2–3], [1–4]
   - This is non-negotiable house style
   - Impact: Complete reformatting required; violation risks desk rejection

3. ABSTRACT STRUCTURE [ENFORCED]
   - Field baseline: Flowing prose paragraph(s), no mandatory labeling
   - EJHE practice: Explicit labeled sections (Objective|Methods|Results|Conclusions)
   - Impact: Free-form abstracts rejected in initial screening

4. SECTION ORDERING [ENFORCED]
   - Field baseline: Flexible (optional Background, optional standalone Literature, Results/Discussion merged)
   - EJHE practice: Rigid IMRAD order (no flexibility in sequencing)
   - Impact: Attempting variable section order fails editorial screening

5. HEDGING INTENSITY [PRACTICE ONLY, RELAXATION]
   - Field baseline: Low hedging; clear claims preferred
   - EJHE practice: More frequent qualifiers ("suggests," "appears," "do not contradict")
   - Reflects biomedical observational caution, not identification-driven confidence
   - Impact: Mild relaxation from field norm; acceptable but not required
