4. Methodological Transparency — DELTA
   - Stronger emphasis than quantitative finance baseline
   - Explicit documentation of qualitative approach (phenomenology, case study, etc.)

   - Less formulaic contribution enumeration

6. Results and Interpretation Boundary — DELTA
