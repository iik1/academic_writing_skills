KEY FINDINGS SUMMARY

Financial History Review is a disciplined, tightly-scoped journal that maintains the narrative-analytical character of business and economic history while imposing strict length and formatting constraints appropriate to an ABS 2 publication. The most salient difference from the field baseline is the mandatory word limit (~8,000 words including all supporting material) and the restriction of footnotes to source citation only, both of which enforce more linear, compressed argumentation than some field journals permit.
