DELTAS FOUND
============
Total deltas identified: 5 major categories

1. **Length Constraint (ENFORCED):** 30-page maximum (including abstract, tables, figures), double-spaced. FIELD_SKILL does not specify a universal page limit. This is a hard constraint.

2. **Citation Style (ENFORCED):** Chicago Manual of Style 16th edition, author-date system (not APA). This is journal-specific and non-negotiable.

3. **Accessibility Requirement (ENFORCED, RELAXATION):** Explicit editorial criterion requiring "free from disciplinary jargon and methodological detail only of interest to people with advanced specialized training." Significant relaxation from FIELD_SKILL baseline, which tolerates formal-dense register and higher theoretical complexity. Practitioner accessibility is primary evaluation criterion.

4. **Theoretical/Methodological Emphasis (RELAXATION):** "Novelty of topic, clarity of presentation, and accessibility" explicitly prioritized over "theoretical rationale and methodological sophistication" (stated in UALE guidelines). Econometric rigor and complex modeling are not required. This inverts FIELD_SKILL hierarchy.

5. **Table/Figure Placement (ENFORCED):** Tables and figures numbered separately, placed at manuscript end on separate sheets. Stricter than some HRM journals' practice of inline placement.

FIELD_SKILL OVERRIDES
=====================
FIELD_SKILL baseline (from ABS 4*/4 journals) assumes formal, theoretically dense, methodologically rigorous register. LSJ overrides this on:
- Register/accessibility: Simplify language, remove jargon
- Citation density: Accept papers with novelty-of-data focus over theoretical density
- Methodological exposition: Simplify for general audience, do not assume specialist training
- Page budget: Strict 30-page limit requires tight writing
