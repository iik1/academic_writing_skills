DELTAS FOUND: 6 substantive deltas + 1 structural constraint

1. SUBMISSION MODEL (STRUCTURAL)
   - Unsolicited manuscripts rejected; invitation-only editorial committee selection.
   - This is not a writing delta but a gatekeeping delta. Critical for researchers to understand.
   - Classification: ENFORCED structural constraint (overrides all writing advice if not invited)

2. ABSTRACT LENGTH LIMIT (HARD CONSTRAINT)
   - 150 words maximum (enforced in author handbook and observed across all sampled abstracts)
   - FIELD_SKILL does not specify abstract length
   - Classification: ENFORCED delta (significantly shorter than many ABS 4*/4 journals)

3. MANDATORY SUMMARY POINTS AND FUTURE ISSUES (HARD CONSTRAINT)
   - Summary Points: up to 8 key conclusions in complete sentences
   - Future Issues: up to 8 forward-looking directions
   - Both positioned above Literature Cited section
   - Not mentioned in FIELD_SKILL
   - Classification: ENFORCED (journal-specific mandatory components)

4. REFERENCE ANNOTATIONS (OPTIONAL BUT STANDARD)
   - Brief explanations of citation importance (15 words maximum per annotation)
   - Not mentioned in FIELD_SKILL
   - Classification: PRACTICE ONLY (observed in author guidelines; prevalence not verified in articles due to paywall)

5. SECTION TITLE: "LITERATURE CITED" (HARD CONSTRAINT)
   - Must use "Literature Cited" not "References"
   - Follows Harvard author-year format
   - Citation format itself aligns with FIELD_SKILL
   - Classification: ENFORCED (naming convention delta)

6. ACCESSIBILITY/VOICE RELAXATION (INFERRED)
   - Articles are pitched to resource-economics practitioners and policy makers, not pure economic theorists
   - Formality remains high (per FIELD_SKILL) but language is slightly less technically dense than ABS 4*/4
   - Consistent with ABS 2 tier (lower tier = more accessible)
   - Classification: RELAXATION (not a violation of FIELD_SKILL, but a systematic easing of top-tier intensity)

FIELD_SKILL OVERRIDES
====================

The journal's invitation-only model, while not a direct writing override, completely restructures how FIELD_SKILL
applies. Authors cannot apply FIELD_SKILL proactively; they may only apply it reactively (after receiving
commission from editorial committee). This is unique among journal-specific skills and is documented prominently
in the hard constraints section.

All other norms (citation style, voice, hedging logic, sentence structure, positioning relative to literature)
align with or relax FIELD_SKILL norms. No conflicts requiring FIELD_SKILL overrides found.
