DELTAS FOUND
============

Count: 3 distinct dimensions with journal-specific deviations or additions

1. Ethical Declarations Section (HARD ENFORCED ADDITION)
   - Classification: ENFORCED (stated in guidelines, confirmed in article metadata)
   - Delta: FIELD_SKILL does not prescribe formal ethics/conflict-of-interest declarations.
     Journal requires a separate "Declarations" section (on separate title page or subsection)
     listing: funding, competing interests, ethics approval, consent, data availability,
     authors' contributions. This is addition, not relaxation.

2. Supplementary Materials Terminology (PRACTICE ONLY)
   - Classification: PRACTICE ONLY (observed in article numbering conventions, not found in
     publicly accessible guidelines)
   - Delta: Journal uses "Online Resource" for supplementary files; maintains separate figure
     numbering for online appendices (main text figures numbered sequentially, online appendix
     figures numbered independently). FIELD_SKILL does not specify this terminology.

3. Abstract Prohibition on Undefined Abbreviations and References (ENFORCED)
   - Classification: ENFORCED (stated in guidelines)
   - Delta: FIELD_SKILL does not explicitly prohibit undefined abbreviations or unspecified
     references in abstracts. Journal of International Entrepreneurship explicitly states both are
     forbidden in the 150–250-word abstract. Minor addition to field norm but explicitly stated.

All other dimensions (voice/register, citation density, results reporting, discussion structure,
hedging, argumentation patterns, methods transparency) show no deviation from FIELD_SKILL.
Sample articles follow field baseline conventions for first-person plural, active voice,
high citation density in intro/theory, hypothesis-by-hypothesis results reporting, and
discussion subsections (Contributions, Implications, Limitations).
