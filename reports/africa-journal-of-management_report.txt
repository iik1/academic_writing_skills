OBSERVED DELTAS FROM FIELD_SKILL
================================================================================

1. AFRICA-CENTRIC AND INDIGENOUS KNOWLEDGE INTEGRATION (PRACTICE ONLY / JOURNAL EMPHASIS)
   Classification: PRACTICE ONLY (observed emphasis; not a format delta; not contradicted by field baseline)

   The journal's charter explicitly requires research that "links" Western management theories with Africa's indigenous knowledge systems, wisdom, and current realities. This is a **thematic/scope delta**, not a format delta:

   - Articles must center Africa's institutional, economic, cultural, environmental realities as PRIMARY focus, not as a test case for Western theories
   - Engagement with African philosophical frameworks (e.g., Ubuntu) is encouraged and expected when relevant
   - Comparative or integrative theoretical framing (West-Africa) is valued
   - MGMT field baseline already supports this; AJOM simply emphasizes it as a requirement

   Impact on writer: Topic selection and theoretical framing must explicitly position Africa as focal point. Contextual detail is not optional.

2. ARTICLE TYPE DISTRIBUTION (PRACTICE ONLY / JOURNAL EMPHASIS)
   Classification: PRACTICE ONLY (observed pattern; field supports it; no contradiction)

   All accessible articles were integrative/systematic reviews. This is not a structural requirement (field baseline supports reviews) but may reflect editorial preference for synthesis-driven work.

   Impact on writer: If submitting an empirical study, position it within broader African management literature and synthesis agenda.

3. METHODOLOGICAL FLEXIBILITY (RELAXATION of ABS 4*/4 baseline)
   Classification: RELAXATION (lower-tier journal; pragmatic about design constraints in African contexts)

   As an ABS 2 journal, AJOM explicitly welcomes:
   - Mixed-methods approaches that incorporate African perspectives
   - Qualitative, ethnographic, and exploratory designs
   - No evidence of strict identification strategy requirements (IV, diff-in-diff, PSM) as enforced in top-tier outlets

   MGMT field baseline emphasizes robust identification; AJOM is more accommodating of contextual constraints and exploratory work.

   Impact on writer: If using observational, case-based, or exploratory designs, frame them as appropriate to African institutional contexts, not as design limitations.
